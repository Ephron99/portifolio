Place your CV here
------------------

Save your CV PDF in this folder as:

    Ephron_Twagirimana_CV.pdf

The "Download CV" button in the hero section links to /cv/Ephron_Twagirimana_CV.pdf.
If you use a different file name, update profile.cv.path (and label) in
src/data/profile.js.

Until a real PDF exists here, the Download CV button shows a setup notice
instead of a broken download — no fake files are served.
